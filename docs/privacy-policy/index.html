Privacy

Privacy policy

Plain-language summary first, full policy second. Last updated 22 September 2026.

The short version

We collect the minimum needed to run this site. We do not sell personal data. We use cookies for analytics and, where enabled, advertising. You can opt out at any time. Questions go to privacy@aitoolscout.com.

1. Who we are

AI Tool Scout ("we", "us", "our") operates this website. For data protection purposes, we are the data controller of the personal data described in this policy. Contact: privacy@aitoolscout.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact form: name, email address and message, if you choose to send one.
  • Newsletter: email address, if you subscribe. You can unsubscribe from any email we send.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: pages visited, referring page, approximate location (country/city level), device and browser type.
  • Technical data: IP address, which we process for security and abuse prevention and then minimise or aggregate.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

We use the following categories of cookie:

TypePurposeOpt out
Strictly necessarySecurity, load balancing, remembering your consent choiceNot possible
AnalyticsAggregated statistics about how the site is usedCookie banner / browser settings
AdvertisingRelevant advertising and measuring ad performanceCookie banner / Google Ads Settings

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect how the site works.

4. Analytics

We use privacy-conscious, aggregated analytics to understand which articles are useful. Analytics data is processed in aggregate where possible. Where we use Google Analytics, Google acts as a processor and its own terms apply; you can opt out via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Advertising and Google AdSense

We may display advertising, including through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this and other sites.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of third-party vendor cookies at AboutAds.info or YourAdChoices.
  • Google's own handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy & Terms policy.

Advertising does not give Google access to information you have provided directly to us.

6. How we use your information

  • To respond to messages you send us.
  • To send a newsletter you subscribed to.
  • To operate, secure and improve the website.
  • To measure which content is useful.
  • To display and measure advertising, where enabled.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

7. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Consent — non-essential cookies and newsletter sign-up.
  • Legitimate interests — site security, aggregated analytics, improving content, contextual advertising.
  • Contract — responding to a request you made.
  • Legal obligation — tax, accounting or regulatory requests.

8. Sharing your information

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who host the site, deliver email or provide analytics, under contracts restricting their use of your data.
  • Advertising partners, including Google, where advertising is enabled and you have consented.
  • Authorities, where legally required.

9. Retention

Contact messages are kept for up to 24 months. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is retained in aggregated form. Advertising data follows Google's own retention periods.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our use of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. You may also lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

To exercise any right, email privacy@aitoolscout.com. We respond within 30 days.

11. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

12. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date below. Material changes will be highlighted prominently.

13. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@aitoolscout.com. General contact via the contact page.

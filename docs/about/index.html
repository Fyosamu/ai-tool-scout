About

About us

We test AI tools the way you would — with real work, real deadlines, and no vendor input.

Our mission

AI Tool Scout exists to answer one question honestly: is this tool actually worth your money and your time?

The internet is full of AI tool roundups written by people who have used each product once, or not at all — often with affiliate links pointing to whichever tool pays the highest commission. We do things differently. Every tool in our comparisons is used for real work over multiple weeks before we publish a word.

How we test

Our process is deliberately boring and repeatable:

  1. Same brief for everyone. Every tool in a comparison receives identical input, identical constraints and an identical number of attempts.
  2. Blind scoring. Where possible, editors score drafts without knowing which tool produced them.
  3. Measure editing time. The real cost of an AI tool is not its subscription — it is how long you spend fixing its output. We time that.
  4. Check the fine print. Pricing, usage limits, data policies and commercial rights are verified against the provider's own terms, not its marketing page.
  5. Re-test on major releases. These products change monthly. When a significant release ships, we re-run the relevant tests and update the article with a dated changelog.
What we do not do

We do not accept payment for a review, a score, or a placement. We do not publish scores we have not earned through testing. If a tool is bad, we say so — including tools we link to.

How we make money

AI Tool Scout is free to read and carries no paywall. We earn revenue in two ways:

  • Affiliate commissions. Some links to third-party tools earn us a commission if you subscribe. This never affects a price you pay, a score, or whether a tool appears in a comparison.
  • Advertising. We display contextual advertising. Ads are clearly marked and never placed inside our editorial verdicts.

Both revenue streams are capped by one rule: the reader's trust is the only asset we actually own. A higher score cannot be bought.

Editorial standards

  • Claims are sourced, or marked as our own opinion.
  • Pricing and limits are verified at the time of publication.
  • Corrections are published openly at the bottom of an article, not silently applied.
  • Every article is written or reviewed by a named human being.

Who we are

We are a small, independent editorial team of writers, developers and analysts who use these tools daily in our own work. We have no investors, no parent media company, and no obligation to any vendor.

If you spot an error, an outdated price, or a claim you think we got wrong, please tell us. Corrections improve the site for everyone, and we will credit the change.

About

About us

We research AI tools the way a careful buyer would — primary sources first, and no vendor input.

Our mission

AI Tool Scout exists to answer one question honestly: is this tool actually worth your money and your time?

The internet is full of AI tool roundups written by people who have used each product once, or not at all — often with affiliate links pointing to whichever tool pays the highest commission. We do things differently. Every tool in our comparisons is checked against its own pricing page, documentation, changelog and terms before we publish a word — and scored against a fixed rubric, not a feature checklist.

How we research

Our process is deliberately boring and repeatable:

  1. Primary sources first. Pricing, usage limits, data policies and commercial rights are taken from the provider's own terms and documentation, not its marketing page.
  2. Independent evidence. Where reputable third-party benchmarks or tests exist, we use them and say so — instead of inventing numbers of our own.
  3. One rubric for every tool. Each tool in a comparison is scored on the same criteria: output quality, instruction following, reliability, workflow fit, and price against value.
  4. Opinion marked as opinion. When a judgment is ours rather than a documented fact, the text says so. Scores are editorial ratings, explained in the article itself.
  5. Re-check on major releases. These products change monthly. When a significant release ships, we re-check the affected claims and update the article with a dated changelog.
What we do not do

We do not accept payment for a review, a score, or a placement. We do not publish a score we cannot justify against our published criteria. If a tool is bad, we say so — including tools we link to.

How we make money

AI Tool Scout is free to read and carries no paywall. We earn revenue in two ways:

  • Affiliate commissions. Some links to third-party tools earn us a commission if you subscribe. This never affects a price you pay, a score, or whether a tool appears in a comparison.
  • Advertising. We display contextual advertising. Ads are clearly marked and never placed inside our editorial verdicts.

Both revenue streams are capped by one rule: the reader's trust is the only asset we actually own. A higher score cannot be bought.

Editorial standards

  • Claims are sourced, or marked as our own opinion.
  • Pricing and limits are verified at the time of publication.
  • Corrections are published openly at the bottom of an article, not silently applied.
  • Every article carries a named author and a visible update history — the people behind those bylines, their backgrounds and how we work are set out on the editorial team page.

Who we are

We are a small, independent editorial team of writers, developers and analysts who use these tools daily in our own work. We have no investors, no parent media company, and no obligation to any vendor.

If you spot an error, an outdated price, or a claim you think we got wrong, please tell us. Corrections improve the site for everyone, and we will credit the change.
